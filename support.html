Support

Privacy questions and support: support.actum@gmail.com.

actum is developed by Isaac Abraham.

For beta feedback, open TestFlight, select actum, and choose Send Beta Feedback. You can report a bug or ask a privacy question. Apple automatically shares TestFlight crash reports with the developer.

Describe what happened, what you expected, and your app and iOS versions. Remove private calendar links and sensitive content before sending screenshots or logs.

Your productivity database is stored locally. The developer cannot retrieve or remotely erase it. Delete App in iOS Settings removes local app data; Offload App keeps it. Manage backup copies separately through Apple backup settings.

If your data cannot be opened, do not delete the app. Retry after unlocking the device and checking storage. The recovery screen can prepare a local copy for Files or sharing; Support can export files preserved by older app versions. A recovery copy contains private data and does not automatically restore anything.

Revoking Calendar or Reminders permission stops future access but keeps earlier imported copies. Saved briefings are separate records and can still mention edited or deleted items.

Open the app regularly to refill its reminder queue. Use Retry reminders in Settings if scheduling fails. Focus and system notification settings can affect alert delivery.
