Privacy Policy

actum · Last updated October 24, 2026

actum is developed by Isaac Abraham. This policy describes the current iPhone app, including beta versions distributed through TestFlight.

Your productivity data

The app stores your tasks, events, habits, goals, milestones, notes, dates, locations you enter, linked items, completion history, imported calendar data, settings, and saved briefings in its local app storage. Appearance preferences, welcome-screen completion, and the data-recovery notice use local preferences.

The app has no user accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server. The app does not upload your productivity database to the developer. It does not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Siri, Shortcuts, and Search

Siri & Search is enabled by default and can be turned off in actum Settings. It makes your item titles, notes, dates, locations, goal and milestone relationships, progress, schedules, and completion history available through Apple’s App Intents and Spotlight system index. Archived items and saved completion history can be searchable. Raw calendar-import payloads, feed tokens, and source identifiers are excluded from this integration.

Apple controls how Siri, Spotlight, Shortcuts, and supported Apple Intelligence features use this information, subject to your device, language, region, and Apple system settings. This is separate from actum’s on-device briefing generation. actum does not send this data to a developer-operated server. Siri actions that read your data require local device authentication. Shortcuts can pass information to other actions or apps when you use them.

Turning off Siri & Search disables actum’s queries and requests removal of its indexed entities. Saving or deleting an item schedules a search-index refresh; system search can briefly show older information until indexing finishes. Open actum or use Refresh search index in Settings to retry. Turning this off does not erase your productivity records or revoke information you already passed to another app through a shortcut.

Apple Calendar and Reminders

Importing from Apple Calendar or Reminders is optional. The app asks for the relevant system permission when you choose an import. You can use the other features without granting it.

Imports create local copies. They do not update or delete items in Apple Calendar or Reminders. Imported information may include titles, notes, locations, dates, repeat rules, completion state, and source identifiers. Revoking access stops future access; it does not delete copies already imported into the app.

Calendar links and network requests

When you choose to import an HTTPS or webcal calendar link, the app downloads that calendar over HTTPS. The selected calendar provider, its hosting providers, and any host reached through a redirect receive the requested URL, including any access token in it, your IP address, and ordinary network-request information. Their handling of request logs is governed by their policies. The app does not send your existing task or habit database to that provider.

The download uses a temporary network session without persistent cookie or response caching. The app does not save the calendar subscription URL as a setting or log it. It stores a hash of the feed URL to recognize later imports, together with event identifiers and imported content. Original event properties are retained locally, including properties the interface does not display; those properties can contain names, email addresses, links, or other information included by your calendar provider.

Importing is manual. The app does not subscribe to the feed or refresh it in the background. Treat private calendar links as passwords and avoid including them in support requests or screenshots.

Briefings and Apple Intelligence

Briefings are generated using Apple's on-device model on supported devices, or a built-in template. This version does not call Private Cloud Compute or an external AI service. Relevant app information is processed locally to write the briefing, and the resulting text is saved locally.

You can turn AI summaries off in Settings. That switches to the built-in template and regenerates the current briefing when Today refreshes. Other saved briefings remain in local storage. Generated summaries can contain mistakes; check important details against your items.

Notifications

Reminders and briefing alerts are scheduled locally through Apple's notification system. Alerts can contain an item's title and timing information. Depending on your system settings, this information may appear on the Lock Screen or connected notification surfaces. Choose whether to show notification previews in iOS Settings.

You can change individual reminders, turn notification categories off inside the app, or change notification permission in iOS Settings. Category switches preserve each item's reminder choices so you can enable the category again later.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Your local data remains until you delete it. Archiving hides an item while preserving its data and history. Permanent deletion removes the item and associated records according to its relationships; other linked items can remain. Deleting an imported item does not delete the original from its calendar provider, and importing again can recreate the local copy.

Saved briefing text is a separate local record and may still mention items you later edit or delete. Regenerating a briefing replaces that day and kind's text; older saved briefings are retained. Turning AI off or revoking Calendar or Reminders permission does not erase earlier saved data.

If the app cannot open its database, it shows a recovery screen instead of resetting your files or starting with an empty store. You can retry opening the data or prepare a recovery copy. Older app versions may have preserved files in a local recovery folder; those copies can be exported from Support. This is not an automatic restore service.

Preparing a recovery copy creates a temporary ZIP on your device containing the preserved database files, which can include private productivity information. It does not send the copy to the developer. You choose whether to save it to Files or share it using the system share sheet; your selected destination controls any resulting transfer or cloud storage. The app removes its temporary export when you leave the export screen. Original files and any copies you saved elsewhere remain until you delete them.

To remove all local app data, including saved briefings, local preferences, and recovery files, use Delete App in iOS Settings. Offload App keeps documents and data. Your device or computer backups may include app data depending on your backup settings. The app has no cross-device sync service; device backup and restore are separate Apple-managed features. Manage or delete backup copies using Apple's backup controls. Deleting the app does not delete items in Apple Calendar, Reminders, or your calendar provider.

Support, diagnostics, and TestFlight

If you contact the developer, information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or logs, is used to respond and troubleshoot. Send only what is needed and remove private calendar links and sensitive content first. Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve the request, provide reasonable follow-up, or meet legal obligations; you can request its deletion.

Apple may provide diagnostics under its platform settings and privacy terms. TestFlight automatically shares crash reports with the developer and lets you submit feedback, screenshots, and comments. Feedback can include your contact information when provided through the invitation or feedback process. These Apple-managed features are separate from the app's code; the app does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Your choices and contact

You can edit or delete your local items, choose which integrations to use, revoke system permissions, turn AI summaries off, manage notification previews, and manage app backups. The developer cannot access, retrieve, or remotely erase your local productivity database. For privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, use Support in the app's Settings.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated when the app's data handling changes. The revision date appears above. Cloud AI, cross-device sync, or any new data collection would need updated disclosures before being introduced.
